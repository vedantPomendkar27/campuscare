Academic Project Reference

Thakur Ramnarayan College of Arts & Commerce

CampusCare is developed as a final year BSc (Computer Science) mini-project by Mr. Vedant Milind Pomendkar (Roll No. 2407106) under the mentorship of Dr. Sandeep Kamble (Head of Department, Computer Science).

  • Full 3-Tier Architecture: Bootstrap 5, Express.js REST API, and Relational 3NF SQL.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Multi-level privileges for Students and Administrators.
  • Resilient Runtime: Native MySQL support with automatic zero-configuration SQLite fallback.
